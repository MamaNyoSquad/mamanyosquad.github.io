    <h1>「正義」は生き続ける。</h1>
    <span>5 Jan 2024</span>
    <br><br><br>
    <p>
      This is <b>Emmannuel Ortega</b>, Social &amp; Web Manager for #MamaNyoSquad.
    </p>
    <p>
      We are almost at the end of an era as SINoALICE bids farewell for the final
      moment for everyone playing on the Japanese Edition. We at the Squad had been
      tuning in as the news unfolds to the rest of the nation.
    </p>
    <p>
      As a fellow player, I understood what the others feel as they mourn the loss
      of another great game that has touched the hearts of the many; however, may we
      utilize this moment to cherish our past experiences as we usher forward to the
      brighter future.
    </p>
    <p>
      I would like to extend my thanks to SQUARE ENIX and Pokelabo for keeping the
      spirit alive to its last breath. Without your support, there will be no
      SINoALICE as we know it today. The accolades both of you had shared to the
      players will always linger on forever.
    </p>
    <p>
      To Yoko Taro, who have been the brains behind this project: As Nana Bajo, you
      have my sincerest thanks in everything that you do for SINoALICE. I hope that
      you will be able to usher forward with your new projects as well as
      revolutionizing things that captured everyone's hearts to the fullest.
    </p>
    <p>
      For the final moment, before the Farewell Special: <b>Godspeed, SINoALICE!</b>
    </p>
    <br><br>
