What is this page about?

This page is the home of all of Aga-chuu (G.Mgr)'s playthroughs throughout her journey with DOAXVV (DEAD OR ALIVE Xtreme Venus Vacation) Global.

It covers all of her instances of play time, from the first live-tweet to the most recent VOD recording -- in chronological order.


When did she first played DOAXVV?

26 Nov 2020.


How frequent is this page being updated?

In normal circumstances, this page is updated on a bi-weekly basis (once every two weeks, starting 15 Nov 2026) -- notwithstanding the frequency of updates everywhere on website.

For transparency, related commits will bear the VOD UPDATE label on GitHub.


Some of the VODs were split. What happened?

Since her first series of video playthroughs were raw recordings and that she was unable to edit those at the time, some of the VODs are confirmed split into multiple parts.

Don't worry if you've bumped into one -- they are all consolidated to a single section for ease of access.

(This rule also applies for live-tweets too, as some posts were split post-consolidation.)


Some VODs were missing. What happened?

Some of them were designated as offcams, and the others were streamed live. As such, they are excluded from the main series; but they will reside on their own playlists.


I can't see content for some posts!! (Pre-Session 125)

Some of the quoted posts were age-restricted by Twitter/X, and we have no control over it. Furthermore, you must be logged in in order to see the full thread.

Our apologies for the inconvenience.
